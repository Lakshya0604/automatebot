Setup in 5 steps

About 10 minutes.

  1. Install the app and allow the microphone.
  2. On the TV: Settings, About, tap Build 7 times. Then Developer options, turn on network debugging.
  3. In the app enter the TV's IP address and tap Connect. Accept the prompt on the TV.
  4. Tap Start listening. Say "bot one", wait for the beep, then say a command.
  5. For IR devices: wire an ESP8266 with an IR LED and a receiver (about 100 rupees), then use Learn button in the app.
